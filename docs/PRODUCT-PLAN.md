# oobIT Product Plan

## BHAG
Become a trusted front door for small businesses trying to understand, choose, simplify and use AI and business technology.

## Product thesis
Start with the human situation rather than a software category. Interview for the business problem, desired outcome, current systems and constraints. Route toward the smallest reasonable next step.

## Valid recommendation classes
KEEP, OPTIMIZE, CONNECT, ADD, REPLACE, REFER, OOB, DO NOTHING.

## Product principles
1. Best fit before compensation.
2. Human agency before conversion.
3. Ask only questions that can change the recommendation.
4. Preserve what already works.
5. Prefer less technology when less solves the problem.
6. Keep recommendation evidence current and attributable.
7. Treat emotional recognition as context, not diagnosis.
8. Keep private Project intelligence out of the public runtime.

## Release sequence
- v0.1: browser-based decision interview and transparent rule engine.
- v0.2: persistent anonymous/consented event analytics and structured interview outcomes.
- v0.3: verified solution catalog and source provenance.
- v0.4: vendor/referral routing with explicit disclosure.
- v0.5: voice channel via Twilio + ElevenLabs, orchestration via n8n.
- v0.6: first validated vertical pack for local service businesses.
- v1.0: measured recommendation quality, reliable routing, clear privacy controls and documented maintenance burden.
