# oobIT Downstream Handoff

## Destination and task
- Destination: `rodneyobyrne/oobIT`
- Public front end: `go.oobcreative.com`
- Task: build an interview-driven practical technology decision environment.

## Selected direction
The public system starts with a recognizable business situation, not a software category. It may recommend keeping, optimizing, connecting, adding, replacing, referring, using oob services, or doing nothing.

## Audience
Capable owner-led and small organizations whose business responsibilities have outgrown the systems supporting them. The visitor should feel recognized, capable, in control and relieved—not behind or technically inadequate.

## One thing to understand
You do not need to become a technology expert to run a modern business.

## Required principles
- Go deeper, not louder.
- Technology supports human judgment.
- Best fit before compensation.
- No fear-based or hype-based AI language.
- No invented performance claims.
- Persona names remain internal.

## Exclusions
- Full private Project history.
- Private persona evolution.
- Unverified testimonials, metrics or customer outcomes.
- Paid vendor ranking.
- Broad promise to solve every business problem.

## Initial technical constraints
Static, dependency-light front end; accessible; mobile-first; deterministic and testable first recommendation engine. External AI and vendor integrations follow only after the front-end interaction and data contract are stable.
